<p><span class="dropcap">T</span>here is a story I was telling, and I retold it many times but it has been a while since I told it last. Now getting back to the story I notice some gaps that I do not know how to bridge and I am getting this odd feeling that perhaps this never happened. I stop myself at that point and remind myself that I don't make up such things, that I have no reason to and that I find enough interest and weirdness in reality to come up with such a thing. Well, here is the story:
</p>
<p>It was a city, I don't know which, and some part of the city which I don't know. I ended up in that part and my glasses frame broke and so I found this optician shop, I remember it more like a hardware shop with a counter and a man in the counter that fetches what you want and the counter was more like a counter behind a window that he can close. I am not sure what kind of an optician has a setup like that.. I handed him my glases and asked when it will be ready as I will just be hanging outside till it's ready, and he said an hour so I went across the road to a park and sat on a bench. I can't see a thing without my glasses, I am not sure how I passed the hour. When the hour was gone I went over to the counter, the old man looked at me and said `Hello there, how can I help you?`, I told him I left him my glasses to fix an hour ago and he went to a back room for awhile. When he came back, he looked at me and said `Hello there, how can I help you`... So this is the strange story, he gave me my glasses after that not sure how he did come to remember.  
</p>
{{< img src="images/issue_013/10.jpg" alt="Masked" caption="Masked" class="center-image" >}}
<p>This time round telling the story caused me great embarrassment. As I was telling it I saw it made no sense, and I had to stop. I remembered how my dad told me a few times that often it happens to him. A story that was told many times and is no longer his. Remembering a retelling and not the memory.</p>

{{< img src="images/issue_013/18.jpg" alt="Rocket" caption="Rocket" class="center-image" >}}
<p> A similar category of un-truths I experienced as a child. Stories of others that appealed to me and I willed them mine. My brother was one such hero, the stories of his wars with the greater neighborhood gangs, the stories of a braver, more solid generation that felt much more valid than our own, five years younger, too late, and so unprepared.</p>

{{< img src="images/issue_013/12.jpg" alt="Do I know you?" caption="Do I know you?" class="center-image" >}}
<p>The images here are from a notebook I started on June 2025 and finished in July 2026, it was hard going. Airports, hospital, death. Memories of places I haven't visited for 25 years, and even when I did visit I never really stopped.

The method of drawing is a bit hardcore. I start not knowing where it is going. I learned not to mind the embarrassment, not to mind people interpretations, their remarks about my subconscious. I feel that the subconscious is common it is not mine.</p>
